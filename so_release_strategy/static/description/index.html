Sales Order Release Strategy

Multi-level approvals for quotations, driven by the sales team. Each team gets a release strategy with amount-based stages, and no order is confirmed until the right people have released it.

Odoo 18.0 Community & Enterprise English & Arabic Technical name: so_release_strategy LGPL-3
1 strategy / team
The sales team decides the approval path, not the salesperson
N stages
Sequential stages, parallel approvers inside a stage
5 statuses
Not Submitted, Waiting Approval, Released, Rejected, No Approval Needed
2 activity types
Approvers are asked to approve; the salesperson is asked to confirm

Why this module

Discount, price and credit decisions need a second pair of eyes before an order is confirmed. Standard Odoo has no release workflow for sales orders.

In standard Odoo 18

  • Any salesperson can press Confirm on a quotation of any amount.
  • There is no approval workflow for sales orders, by amount or by team.
  • Approvers are not told that an order is waiting for them.
  • After confirmation, totals, lines and customer can still change without review.
  • Nothing records who approved what and when.
  • Managers have no list of orders waiting for their approval.

With this module

  • Quotations in a strategy’s amount range go to Waiting Approval instead of being confirmed.
  • Each sales team has its own release strategy with staged rules and amount ranges.
  • Every approver gets a Sales Release Approval activity with a deadline.
  • Locked Fields on a confirmed order either reset the strategy or block the change.
  • A Release Strategy tab and the chatter keep a full audit trail per stage.
  • Sales › Orders › Release Approvals lists exactly the orders waiting for you.

How it works

Four steps from setup to a released order.

1
Enable
Tick Sales Release Strategies in Sales settings.
2
Define
Create a strategy with staged rules and link it to each sales team.
3
Submit
The quotation is checked against its team’s strategy; matching rules create approval lines and activities.
4
Release
After the last stage the order is confirmed automatically, or the salesperson is asked to confirm it.

Features

Screenshots use the Odoo demo database: strategy team one with a team lead (Marc Demo) and a manager (Mitchell Admin) for orders between 1,000 and 10,000 SR untaxed.

Menus & settings

One switch turns release strategies on

Sales › Configuration › Settings › Release Strategies › Sales Release Strategies. Once enabled, every sales team must have a default release strategy, and Sales Team and Salesperson become mandatory on quotations; the team must be one the salesperson belongs to. Shortcuts open Release Strategies and Sales Teams. New menus: Sales › Orders › Release Approvals and Sales › Configuration › Release Strategies.

Feature 1

Release strategies with staged, amount-based rules

Choose the Amount Basis (Total (taxes included) or Untaxed Amount, in company currency), an Approval Deadline (Days) for the activities, and what happens After Final Approval: Confirm the order automatically or Notify the salesperson to confirm manually. Each Approval Rule has a Stage, a Role, an approver (Specific User or User Group) and a Minimum / Maximum Amount. Smart buttons show the linked Sales Teams and Orders.

Rule: a rule applies when the order amount is between its minimum and maximum; a maximum of 0 means no upper limit. Rules with the same stage are approved in parallel; the next stage starts when the current one is complete. Orders that match no rule get No Approval Needed and are confirmed directly. Example: S00046 at 1,770 SR goes to the team lead (stage 1), then the manager (stage 2); S00045 at 295 SR is below 1,000 SR and needs no approval.
Feature 2

Locked fields protect confirmed orders

List the order fields that matter in Locked Fields (for example Total, Order Lines, Customer). Under Changes on Confirmed Orders, choose Reset the release strategy – the order is re-evaluated against its team’s strategy, approvals are cleared and all approvers are notified again – or Block the modification. Changing the sales team or company counts too.

Feature 3

The sales team picks the strategy

Each sales team has a Release Strategy field, also shown as a column in Sales › Configuration › Sales Teams. Salespeople never choose a strategy on the order: the quotation shows its Team Release Strategy and uses it automatically.

Feature 4

Release status on every quotation

Quotation and order lists get a colored Release Status column. New filters: My Release Approvals, Waiting Release, Released and Release Rejected, plus grouping by release status and strategy.

Feature 5

Waiting Approval step and Release Strategy tab

The order status bar gets a Waiting Approval step between Quotation Sent and Sales Order. Approvers see Approve and Reject buttons; supervisors can Bypass or Reset. The Release Strategy tab shows Release Status, Applied Release Strategy, Release Amount, Submitted On, Released On and one line per approver with Stage, Role, State, Assigned On, Approved On, Approved By and Approver Notes.

Feature 6

Activities and a full audit trail

Each approver receives a Sales Release Approval activity when their stage starts; when the order is released with manual confirmation, the salesperson gets Confirm Released Sales Order. Submission, each approval, the status change and the release are all logged in the chatter.

Feature 7

Release Approvals: your queue

Sales › Orders › Release Approvals opens with My Release Approvals applied, so approvers see only the orders waiting for them, in list or kanban.

Release statuses

What each status on an order means.

Not Submitted quotation not yet evaluated against the team strategy
Waiting Approval at least one approval line is still pending
Released all stages approved (or bypassed by a supervisor)
Rejected an approver rejected the order
No Approval Needed the amount matches no rule; the order is confirmed directly
Approval line states: Waiting (later stage), Pending Approval (current stage), Approved, Bypassed, Rejected, Cancelled.

Built for real-world corner cases

Details that matter once the workflow is live.

Wrong team on the order
Sales team and salesperson are mandatory, and the team must be one the salesperson belongs to.
Foreign-currency quotations
Thresholds are compared in company currency (Release Amount), whatever the pricelist currency.
Group approvers
A rule can target a User Group; any member can approve and the line shows the approvers.
Urgent orders
A Release Supervisor can Bypass with a reason; Bypassed By and Bypass Reason stay on the order.
Edits after approval
Changing a locked field resets approvals (counted in Release Resets) or is blocked, per strategy.
Invoices and deliveries during re-approval
While a confirmed order waits for a new approval, or the new approval was refused, it cannot be invoiced – down payments included – and deliveries for the added quantities are created only once it is approved again.
Switching the feature off
Approvals still pending are cancelled and their activities removed; the approval history stays on each order and held deliveries are created.
Small orders
Amounts outside every rule get No Approval Needed – no friction for routine sales.
Multi-company
Strategies, rules and approval lines are company-specific, with record rules per company.
Approvers outside Sales
Record rules let approvers open the orders they must approve.
Arabic users
Full Arabic translation (ar_001) with right-to-left layout.
